Safety rules used

NASA's rules (lunar; none set for Mars yet): 2 km/h, up to 8 h out, up to 2 km from base, slopes up to 20°. Paths use only ground Curiosity drove and skip anything steeper than 20°. Slope is from the rover's tilt: under 17° easier (Apollo 15 walked 17°), 17° to 20° harder.
