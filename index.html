Source: Curiosity REMS daily weather, Centro de Astrobiología via NASA. The provider labels this feed “for outreach purposes only”. It is what Curiosity measured at its position on that sol, not a forecast.

Where

Times are estimates. The 2 km/h default is the pace NASA plans lunar walks at (EVA-EXP-0042, section 4.2.1.3.1); NASA has set no figure for Mars, so here it is our assumption. Difficulty comes from how far Curiosity was tilted on each section: under 10° easier, 10° to 20° moderate, 20° or more hard. The 20° mark is the slope NASA's lunar suit is planned to walk on. Rover tilt hints at slope and is not a slope measurement.
